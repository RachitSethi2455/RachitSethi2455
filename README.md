**🧭 [Cost-Aware Model Router](https://github.com/RachitSethi2455/cost-aware-model-router) · [Live demo](https://cost-aware-model-router.vercel.app)**
Sends each LLM request to a cheap or an expensive model and reports every result with its sample size and a 95% interval. A sandboxed calculator makes the small model compute exact answers instead of guessing (**23/40 → 40/40**). Trained on graded outcomes, the router beat my first router on **5 of 6** model pairs it never saw (APGR 0.645 vs 0.597; random routing = 0.5). Live on a new pair it did no better than random, until it learned from 900 of that pair's own outcomes: **0.475 → 0.552** (95% interval 0.514–0.594). Ships as an OpenAI-compatible API with spend guardrails and Prometheus metrics.
`Python` `FastAPI` `scikit-learn` `LLM APIs` `Prometheus` `Docker` `Vercel`

**📊 [Employee Attrition Prediction](https://github.com/RachitSethi2455/employee-attrition-prediction)**
Leak-free scikit-learn pipeline benchmarking 6 classifiers on 74K HR records; tuned gradient boosting reaches **0.85 ROC-AUC** and is well calibrated (ECE 0.02). A fairness audit found single employees wrongly flagged **43%** of the time vs 16% for married ones; group-specific thresholds cut that gap from **27 to 4 points** for 1.5 points of accuracy. SHAP explanations, a Gradio demo and CI-tested code.
`Scikit-learn` `SHAP` `Fairness` `Gradio`

**🎬 [Movie Genre from Posters](https://github.com/RachitSethi2455/movie-genre-poster-classification)**
Transfer learning on movie posters: 4 ImageNet CNNs compared over 5 repeated splits. ResNet50 is best at **69.6% ± 1.2** on 4 genres (2.8× random). Grad-CAM shows where it looks, it is **91% accurate** when at least 80% confident, and half of its errors name a real second genre on IMDb. A Gradio demo classifies any uploaded poster.
`PyTorch` `Grad-CAM` `Gradio`

**💬 [Smart Review Analyzer](https://github.com/RachitSethi2455/smart-review-analyzer)** *(in progress, team of 2)*

**🔍 [Plagiarism Checker](https://github.com/RachitSethi2455/plagiarism-checker)** *(team of 2)*
Scores two documents, or a whole class's submissions, with 8 similarity methods (n-grams, winnowing, LCS, TF-IDF, MiniLM embeddings…) combined by logistic regression, and labels each sentence as copy, light edit, paraphrase or original. Combined F1: **0.964** on Clough documents, **0.818** on MRPC, and **0.718** on PAWS word swaps, where bag-of-words methods are at chance.
`NLP` `Sentence Embeddings` `spaCy` `Streamlit`
